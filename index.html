Privacy Policy – Bouncy Chicken

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bouncy Chicken (the “App”), published by Pontus Abedin (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the App on iOS or Android.

Support

Bug reports, questions, and feedback: support.bouncychicken@gmail.com

Please include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create a separate account with us, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, or other contact details inside the App (except if you choose to contact us separately). On iOS you may optionally use Apple Game Center (an Apple account feature) for leaderboards.

The App may process the following categories of data:

  • Local game data stored only on your device (for example high score, coins, unlocked skins, settings, purchase status such as “ads removed”, and similar progress) via Unity PlayerPrefs.
  • Purchase data related to optional in-app purchases (see section 2). Purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play; the App uses purchase/receipt status to unlock features (for example removing interstitial ads).
  • Game Center data (iOS, optional) such as a Game Center player identifier, display name/alias, and scores submitted to leaderboards, processed through Apple Game Center when you are signed in (see section 3).
  • Advertising and device data collected by our advertising partners (see below), which may include advertising identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS when you allow tracking), device type, OS version, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data.
  • App usage signals related to ads (for example whether an ad was shown or completed) needed to operate interstitial and rewarded ads.

2. In-app purchases

The App may offer optional non-consumable in-app purchases (for example removing interstitial ads). Payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your full payment card details.

The App may store locally on your device that a purchase was completed (for example so interstitial ads stay removed after relaunch) and may verify purchase/receipt status with the platform stores. Restoring purchases on a new device is handled through the store’s restore/purchase history features where available.

3. Game Center and leaderboards (iOS)

On iOS, the App may use Apple Game Center so you can appear on leaderboards. Using Game Center is optional and controlled through your Apple ID / Game Center settings.

When you use Game Center with the App, Apple may process data such as your Game Center player ID, nickname/display name, and scores or rankings. That information is used to operate leaderboards and related Game Center features. We do not use Game Center data to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

You can manage Game Center in iOS Settings (Game Center / your Apple ID settings) or by signing out of Game Center.

4. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource)

The App displays advertisements through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and may mediate ads from other networks configured in that platform. These partners may collect and use device identifiers and related data to deliver, measure, and (where permitted) personalize ads.

If you purchase an offer that removes interstitial ads, interstitial ads are no longer shown; rewarded ads (optional ads you choose to watch for in-game rewards) may still be available.

On iOS, we may request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you decline, personalized advertising based on the IDFA is limited according to Apple’s rules; ads may still be shown in a less personalized form.

You can change tracking preferences in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

For more information about ironSource / LevelPlay privacy practices, see their documentation and privacy policy on the Unity / ironSource websites.

5. How we use information

  • To run the App and save your local progress and settings
  • To process and restore in-app purchases and unlock purchased features
  • To provide optional Game Center leaderboards
  • To show ads and reward users who watch rewarded ads
  • To measure ad performance and keep the App free to play
  • To comply with platform requirements (Apple / Google)

6. Data storage and retention

Game progress and purchase-unlock status are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App typically deletes that local data (store purchase history may still allow restore on reinstall). Advertising partners and Apple/Google may retain data according to their own policies and retention periods. Game Center data is retained under Apple’s Game Center practices.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Data related to advertising may be processed by Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and mediated ad networks as needed to provide ads. Purchase processing is handled by Apple or Google. Game Center leaderboard data is processed by Apple. We may also disclose information if required by law.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information through the App’s advertising partners or platform features, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Your choices

  • Limit ad tracking via your device privacy settings
  • Reset or limit advertising identifiers in system settings
  • Sign out of Game Center or adjust Game Center settings on your device
  • Restore or manage purchases through App Store / Google Play account settings
  • Uninstall the App to remove local game data
  • Contact us with privacy questions (see below)

10. International users

Depending on where you live (including the EEA/UK), advertising partners and platform providers (Apple / Google) may process data based on consent or other legal bases under applicable law. Where required, consent mechanisms provided by the OS or ad mediation platform may be used.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
