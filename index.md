# Privacy Policy – Bouncy Chicken

Last updated: July 2026

## Information We Collect
We do not directly collect personal information. 
Our game uses Unity LevelPlay/ironSource for advertising, 
which may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads.

## Advertising
This app displays ads provided by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource). 
These services may use cookies and similar technologies 
to serve personalized ads. You can opt out via your device settings.

## Data Storage
Game progress (score, coins, unlocked skins) is stored 
locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs.

## Children
This app is not directed at children under 13.
