# Privacy Policy – Bouncy Chicken

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Bouncy Chicken** (the “App”), published by Pontus Abedin (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the App on iOS or Android.

## Support

Bug reports, questions, and feedback: [pabedin@kth.se](mailto:pabedin@kth.se)

Please include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

## 1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, or other contact details inside the App (except if you choose to contact us separately).

The App may process the following categories of data:

- **Local game data** stored only on your device (for example high score, coins, unlocked skins, settings, and similar progress) via Unity PlayerPrefs.
- **Advertising and device data** collected by our advertising partners, which may include advertising identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS when you allow tracking), device type, OS version, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data.
- **App usage signals related to ads** (for example whether an ad was shown or completed) needed to operate interstitial and rewarded ads.

## 2. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource)

The App displays advertisements through **Unity LevelPlay (ironSource)** and may mediate ads from other networks configured in that platform. These partners may collect and use device identifiers and related data to deliver, measure, and (where permitted) personalize ads.

On iOS, we may request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you decline, personalized advertising based on the IDFA is limited according to Apple’s rules; ads may still be shown in a less personalized form.

You can change tracking preferences in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

## 3. How we use information

- To run the App and save your local progress and settings
- To show ads and reward users who watch rewarded ads
- To measure ad performance and keep the App free to play
- To comply with platform requirements (Apple / Google)

## 4. Data storage and retention

Game progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App typically deletes that local data. Advertising partners may retain data according to their own policies and retention periods.

## 5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Data related to advertising may be processed by Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and mediated ad networks as needed to provide ads. We may also disclose information if required by law.

## 6. Children

The App is **not directed at children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information through the App’s advertising partners, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## 7. Your choices

- Limit ad tracking via your device privacy settings
- Reset or limit advertising identifiers in system settings
- Uninstall the App to remove local game data
- Contact us with privacy questions

## 8. International users

Depending on where you live (including the EEA/UK), advertising partners may process data based on consent or other legal bases under applicable law. Where required, consent mechanisms provided by the OS or ad mediation platform may be used.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
